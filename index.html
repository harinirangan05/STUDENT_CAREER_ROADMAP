How would you detect a cycle in a singly linked list with O(1) extra space?

💡 Hint: Think of two runners on a circular track moving at different speeds (Floyd's Cycle-Finding Algorithm).
🎯 Recommended Approach:
Use Floyd's Tortoise and Hare algorithm. Initialize two pointers (slow and fast) at the head. Advance slow by 1 step and fast by 2 steps. If slow == fast, a cycle exists. If fast reaches null, no cycle exists. Time complexity O(N), Space complexity O(1).
